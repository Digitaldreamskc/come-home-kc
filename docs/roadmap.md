## Current limits

- Research directory names have been reviewed for the public projection; employer participation and location verification remain active work. No current jobs or candidate pool size are implied by the starter records.
- The workspace supports record-level search, sorting, filtering, editing and exports. The overview gives a working sequence; a calculated weekly performance dashboard and annual briefing generator are future features.
- Matching is administrator-assisted. There is no automated ranking, messaging, interview scheduling or hiring decision service.
- Account export downloads the currently loaded account records. Complete consent/history export and deletion fulfillment require an operator workflow.
- Event discovery links to external RSVP services. The schema supports event associations; a dedicated association editor is still needed.
- New2KC form responses, private browser changes and Social Balances records are imported only through an explicitly authorized, reviewed process.
- File uploads, automated expiry reminders and a public impact report are future work. Production email, retention and backup procedures need operating decisions before public launch.

## New2KC form integration

## Annual regional talent and community briefing

Build the briefing from information the team can collect and explain: employer conversation themes approved for aggregation, self-reported location/timing/preferences from consenting participants, verified directory coverage, participation counts with clear definitions, and sourced introduction/outcome records. Start with a data dictionary and collection period. Document the sample, missing responses and limits of any comparison.

Companies can receive a concise regional picture of what participating teams are building, the questions people ask about working and living here, and practical gaps employers can address in their own messaging or community participation. Share aggregated findings and approved stories. Define minimum group sizes and suppress identifying combinations before sharing summaries. Confirm the collection quality before promising specific metrics, benchmarks or year-over-year trends.
