# Mary and Jessica's operating guide

Start with employer conversations. Confirm what a team is building in Kansas City, who handles hiring, what information may be shared publicly, and the next useful conversation. Mary and Jessica own relationships, verification and decisions. AI can organize notes and draft follow-ups for review.

## Access and first review

1. Sign in at `/login`, then open Administration from your account. An operator must first assign the administrator role to your confirmed account.
2. Open Employer conversations. The 21 imported relationships retain their private notes, contacts, sources and next actions.
3. Review the organization, choose an owner and a next action, and set a follow-up date. Use Tasks for work that needs its own reminder.
4. Review new submissions in Review queue. Record a decision and a short explanation. An approved submission still needs its corresponding company, resource, event or job record created or updated.

Each section supports search, status filtering, sorting, record details and JSON export. Exports include the entire loaded section, including records hidden by the current filters; store them in the team's private working files. Use the record's details to find its ID when linking related records. Dates use `YYYY-MM-DD`; timestamps use an ISO value such as `2027-02-15T15:00:00Z`. State lists use `MO,KS`.

## Confirm an employer and its account

In Employer directory, review the company description and website. Keep private contacts and conversation notes in Employer conversations. Publish only approved public fields. Research entries remain clearly labeled until verification is complete.

Add each confirmed office in Employer locations with its state and source. Check the verification box only after checking the source. A Missouri headquarters does not imply that every office or job is in Missouri.

For employer account access, confirm the person's authority with the organization. In Employer access, enter the company ID and the confirmed Auth user ID supplied by the account operator, then verify the membership. The company must also be verified. This gives the account access to its own job listings; it grants no candidate directory access.

## Publish a job

1. Review the pending job and confirm the employer, title, description, application link, work arrangement, closing date and any supplied salary range.
2. For on-site or hybrid work, add and verify the actual work location in Job locations. For remote work, confirm the eligible states explicitly.
3. Verify and publish the employer first. Set the job to active only after the required information is complete. If information is missing, keep it pending and create a follow-up task.
4. Close filled positions promptly. The application removes expired listings from public discovery. An employer edit returns a listing to review before it can appear again.

Employer accounts can submit, edit and close their own listings from the account portal. New company information, hiring needs, corrections, partnership inquiries and mixer interest enter the review queue.

## Resources and events

Confirm the resource's service, eligibility and contact link. Record Missouri and/or Kansas in Resource eligibility and verify each entry before publication. For an event, check date, actual location, intended audience, registration link and capacity when supplied. Publish only confirmed events. The event model supports employer and resource associations; editing those associations currently requires an operator.

## Introductions and follow-up

1. Review the candidate's introduction request and the current verified employer need.
2. Write a short rationale using relevant skills, role interests, geography and timing. Review any AI draft yourself.
3. Ask the candidate to approve the specific introduction and the information to share. Confirm that the employer agrees to receive it.
4. Record both approval timestamps, the sharing scope and permission evidence in Introductions. Only then facilitate the introduction through the agreed channel.
5. Record the introduction date, next action and a follow-up task. Update the stage as the conversation progresses.
6. Record outcomes with a source and mark whether they are reported or confirmed. Obtain separate approval before using a result publicly.

This preview records the workflow; it does not send messages or make hiring decisions.

## Profile export and deletion

People can edit their private profile, download the account information loaded in their portal and submit a deletion request. The portal export includes profile, requests and the account's accessible company/job records. For a complete information request, the team also reviews linked introduction records, consent history and audit retention with the database operator.

For deletion, verify the requester, identify linked records, confirm any required retention and agree the completion date. An authorized database operator completes deletion or de-identification across the profile, submissions, introductions, consent records, audit/import copies where applicable, and Auth account/sessions. Record completion privately and notify the person. Approving the queue item alone does not perform deletion.

## Importing an older backup

Export from the browser that holds the actual old-dashboard changes. Follow the [migration guide](migration.md), review a dry-run report, then use Data imports with the authorized JSON file. Confirm every source record is accounted for. Changed existing records remain for review, preserving newer edits. Keep backups and reports containing personal information in private storage.

## Weekly routine

- Review new employer conversations and submissions; choose the next actions Mary and Jessica can complete.
- Check pending verification, job closing dates and overdue tasks.
- Follow up on introductions only where both approvals are recorded.
- Record sourced outcomes and clean up duplicate or incomplete information.
- Use counts from actual records for planning. Annual insights require a separate collection plan, sample description and permission review before promising a metric.
