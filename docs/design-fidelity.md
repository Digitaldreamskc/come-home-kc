# 2KC design source inventory and visual audit

The comparison baseline is the connected application at 5bd725f. The historical visual reference is the open-demo branch at 9ab1114. Only the integrated build uses the validation database. Archive screenshots use the archive’s isolated demonstration mode.

| Experience | Design source | Regression in the connected baseline | Component reuse and connected behavior |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Come Home KC marketplace and opportunity detail | 9ab1114, components/marketplace.tsx, components/tenant-ui.tsx, foundation.css, tenants/back2kc.json | Earlier header, branding and mobile filter treatment diverged. Employer discovery did not lead into the full partnership story. | Restored KC-heart identity, local fonts, button colors, marketplace controls and card layout. Employer cards link to persistent profiles. The existing public catalog, filters and job routes supply the results. |
| Founding Employer Partners and benefits | 9ab1114, components/founding-partner.tsx, app/partners and app/partner-benefits | The connected /partners route returned 404. | Restored both editorial pages, original Kansas City photograph, navigation, button styles and consent form. The inquiry uses the connected intake. Corporate Talent services have their own linked offer. |
| Regional discovery and Corporate Talent Partners | Historical marketplace header and Founding Partner editorial layout | The public directory had sparse navigation between employment, local resources and experiences. | Reused the header and editorial hero, plus links to published employers, dated experiences, New2KC, Jessica’s List and both partnership offers. The corporate offer has no independently approved historic page in the inspected branch. |
| Employer profiles | Founding Partner editorial components and existing /employers/[id] data route | Stories, requests and records appeared in a continuous list. | Shared editorial profile binds the verified employer, roles, stories, experiences and consent-based actions. The regional skyline is labeled Kansas City. Employer-specific imagery still needs approved assets. |
| New2KC | socialbalances.com/new2kc and its existing Google Form | Public discovery lacked the full community path. | Native site and Form retain their own design. Discovery links to both. Response import requires a read-authorized Forms connection and eligible response. |
| Jessica’s List | jessicas-list.mary473407.chatgpt.site and published directory projection | An empty result did not provide a clear way to explore the source directory. | Linked the original directory with explicit empty and unavailable states. Only published recommendations can populate the connected view. |
| Participant account | 32c6739 and subsequent portal changes through 5bd725f, app/portal | Follow-up navigation sat outside the account interface. | Reused private profile, consent, account and opportunity forms. The authenticated user can access the follow-up queue. |
| Jessica’s operating workspace | Come Home KC app/admin/workspace.tsx and existing account-level access at 5bd725f | A wide record selector and a single partnership page mixed priorities, agreements, publishing, evidence and reports. | Retained team records, search, filters and record details. Added a due-task view and contextual partnership sections. The separate Social Balances intelligence workspace remains separately governed. |
| Employer workspace | Existing employer portal and agreement projection at 5bd725f | Employer and staff shared the same long agreement layout. | Role-specific navigation exposes agreement progress, roles, experiences, fulfillment and approved period reports. Staff-only publication, raw evidence and private welcome follow-up stay behind their existing controls. A separately approved executive mockup was not located. |
| KC Capital Atlas | kc-capital-atlas.vercel.app and Digitaldreamskc/kc-capital-atlas | The core requirement is preserving provenance as research enters an approved report. | The original research interface remains at its source. Staff select a source-linked observation and employers read approved snapshots with dates, units and attribution. |
