Do I need to be returning to KC?

No. This explorer welcomes people coming back, people moving here for the first time, and those still considering a move.

Kansas or Kansas City?

KC is a two-state region. This collection emphasizes Overland Park and Johnson County, with guides and connections across the wider metro. Location labels help you tell the difference. The Kansas Home in the Heartland program focuses on relocation to Kansas.

Does this tell me if I qualify for a program?

No. Exploring and making a shortlist do not determine eligibility. Ask the team to confirm local requirements, availability, and any potential support before making plans.
